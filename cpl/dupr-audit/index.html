DUPR Bracket Audit

Players whose DUPR is outside the expected division bracket. Seasons still being played only — a finished season is compared against ratings that have moved on since, which reports post-season drift as an in-season misplacement.

A flag here is not an accusation. Provisional ratings, ratings that moved after the roster locked, and DUPR changes mid-season all put a player outside their bracket without anyone having done anything wrong — and the bracket boundaries are the league's, applied here as published. A delta under 0.050 is within the noise of the rating itself and shown unemphasised for that reason.
